# Feature gaps: curve fitting, graphs and publication figures (F-nn)

Lens: the model library, fit options, graph kinds and figure/export
features. Read-only research on `main` at 4b20864 (2026-11-23). Every
"absent" was grepped in `engine/opendose/` and `web/src/`; the paths and
lines below are the evidence. Ruling applied: exported figures are
scientific output, so every new figure feature here comes with an exact
check in `web/scripts/e2e-figures.mjs` or `e2e-export.mjs` (or
`e2e-tiff.mjs`).

## What is already there (so not listed)

- **Model library** (`engine/opendose/equations.py` and `nlfit.py`, 91
  registrations). It covers dose-response stimulation and inhibition
  (normalised, variable slope, X as log or concentration), 3PL, 4PL and
  5PL, biphasic, bell-shaped, ECanything and absolute IC50, the operational
  model (depletion, partial agonist), EC50 shift and allosteric shift, and
  Gaddum/Schild. Binding: saturation (total, nonspecific, ligand depletion,
  two sites, Hill, allosteric), competition (one site, two sites, Ki, Ki
  with depletion, homologous) and kinetics (association, dissociation,
  association then dissociation, Motulsky-Mahan). Enzymes:
  Michaelis-Menten, kcat, allosteric sigmoidal, substrate inhibition,
  Morrison, and competitive, noncompetitive, uncompetitive and mixed
  inhibition (global). Also exponentials up to three phases with plateaus,
  lines (segmental, hinge, two intersecting lines, semilog and log-log),
  polynomials up to tenth order (plain and centred), Gaussian, lognormal,
  Lorentzian, sines, growth (logistic, Gompertz, Richards with lag in the
  growth assay), the linear-quadratic model, Boltzmann and power series.
  Quantal probit, logit and cloglog fits with relative potency are in
  `quantal.py`.
- **Fit options.** Hold-constant constraints, weighting (1/Y, 1/Y², 1/X,
  1/X², 1/SD²) with a choice of weight source, asymptotic or profile CIs,
  robust fitting and ROUT, global fits with shared parameters and
  per-data-set constants, user-defined equations (range constraints,
  initial-value rules, transforms to report), confidence and prediction
  bands, the replicates, runs and residual-normality tests, Hougaard
  skewness, range flags ("> top dose"), and fits to mean/SD/N. Compare
  fits handles two models (F test and AICc), one curve against separate
  curves, and one parameter between data sets (ratio with CI). A straight
  line's Slope can also be compared there, which covers "are the slopes
  equal".
- **Graphs and figures.** The Format graph and Format axes layer
  (`web/src/graph/README.md`), brackets and compact letters, the classic
  theme, CVD and grayscale checks, palettes checked by a validator, one
  colour per group across the project (`graph/nameColors.ts`), SuperPlots,
  estimation plots, volcano plots and clustered heat maps, KM with censor
  marks, the at-risk table and CI bands, layouts with panel letters,
  export to PNG, SVG, JPEG, WebP, PDF and TIFF (DPI written into the
  file), .pptx, a font-floor check and the source-data export.

## Not feasible or out of scope

- **EPS, EMF and CMYK output.** Already ruled out of scope (ROADMAP,
  user-guide review). A CMYK conversion without the printer's ICC profile
  changes colours in a way we cannot verify, and journals accept RGB TIFF
  and PDF.
- **Embedding Arial or Helvetica themselves.** Their licences forbid
  redistribution. F-29 and F-30 must use metric-compatible open fonts
  (Liberation Sans or Arimo, OFL/Apache) or Inter.
- **Full LaTeX/MathJax typesetting in labels.** It is heavy, and Plotly's
  MathJax does not survive `toImage`/svg2pdf reliably. The rich-text
  subset in F-28 covers what figure labels need.
- **Bayesian curve fitting with MCMC and priors.** Stan and PyMC are not
  in the Pyodide lock, long chains block a tab, and priors cut against
  "paste the numbers, get the right fit".
- **Band and lane detection on gel images.** It stays in Fiji/ImageJ by
  design (ROADMAP Wave 4 #35). Only provenance is in scope.
- **3D surface fits (Z = f(X, Y)) and 3D graphs** beyond the synergy
  landscapes. The table model has one X column, and demand is low.
- **Live (OLE) links to Word or PowerPoint, and a free-form vector editor**
  inside the app. The .pptx export, "Copy for Word" and layouts cover the
  need.
