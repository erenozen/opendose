Seven read-only audits ran on 2026-11-22 against commit fdbfe9d (same UI as
177053f): emil-prep-for-prod in `audit` mode with its four lane agents
(A+D, B+E+F, C, G+H+I), emil-ui-review on six surfaces (two agents), and
improve-animations plus find-animation-opportunities. Every row uses the
ruling 12 format. The lead vetted every row: a script checked that each
row's verbatim Before excerpt sits on its cited file:line (203 of 203
matched), and the lead re-read the blockers and the clusters below in the
code.

**prep-for-prod verdict: Not yet.** 26 blocker rows across lanes and reviews, which collapse to 10
root causes.

FR 12, GF 16, MO 14, plus 6 motion opportunities). Many rows are the same
root cause seen from two lanes; the cluster table is the work list, the

## Lead vetting notes
- GHI-03 (no description) is downgraded to SHOULD: the page has a title, and
  prep-for-prod's table lists a missing description/OG image as SHOULD.
  GHI-02 stays BLOCKER (a template asset shipped to every tab: "fake content").
- AD-01 (focus return) is inferred from React's unmount order; review-A
  confirmed focus loss on Analyze-menu Escape and note folding in a browser.
  Phase 3 verifies AD-01 in Playwright before and after the Dialog rewrite.
- Plan section 1 claims corrected by the audit: parameter panels apply every
  change immediately, so "no `<form>`, Enter commits nothing" is not a
  defect there (AD vetted-not-reported); about 85 of the 240 `title=`
  attributes are native tooltips, the rest are component props. The grid
  is virtualised and jspdf/svg2pdf/plotly are already dynamic imports.
- The accent focus ring measures at least 3:1 and APCA Lc 30 on every
  surface in both themes (AD contrast matrix), so ruling 5 keeps it.
- Dark `--page` #000 (GHI-15) is a ruling 11 direction item, decided in
  DECISIONS.md, not a defect.

- `web/src/guide/StartScreen.tsx:160` staggered entrance of the table-type cards. **Rejected on function: a launcher the user wants to act on at once; a stagger across about ten cards delays reading the grid, and the delight budget is already spent on the engine-ready reveal.**

- Swipe-to-close on the phone navigator drawer. **Rejected: no drag seam exists; this is a new gesture feature, not a missing bridge.**

#### Part 3: Verdict

OpenDose needs very little new motion. Its existing motion is already restrained and mostly well tuned (strong ease-out tokens, `@starting-style` entrances, nothing on the grid). The work is corrective (MO-01 to MO-10), not additive. Of the additions, O-03 (the Toast's enter and exit) has the highest leverage because every save, copy and error passes through it. It belongs in the phase 3 Toast primitive. To turn any row into a plan, run `improve-animations plan <row>`.
